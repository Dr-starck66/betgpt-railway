# BETGPT ASTRA Ω — ROI5 Dominance + Maturity Gate V1

Date: 2026-09-27

## Objective
Increase net betting ROI by rejecting ambiguous 1X2 side bets rather than increasing stake size or leverage. The target was an additional +5 percentage points on a chronological validation slice while keeping drawdown controlled.

## Candidates rejected before integration
Two candidate filters were tested and rejected because their apparent development improvement did not survive the later chronological check:
- rolling recent calibration gate;
- recent-form double-confirmation gate.

They are not active in the production pipeline.

## Chosen rule
A 1X2 home/away BET is accepted only when both conditions are met:
1. **Dominance margin >= 0.08**: selected side probability minus the second-highest 1X2 probability is at least 8 percentage points.
2. **League segment maturity >= 15**: at least 15 earlier settled, dominance-qualified observations exist in the same league before kickoff.

The gate never reads the future match result. The outcome of earlier rows is used only to establish that those observations are already settled; win/loss is not used to decide whether a historical observation counts toward maturity.

If either rule fails, the decision becomes `NO_BET`, stake is forced to 0, premium status is removed, and any cover is removed. As with the existing XG/0-0 veto, NO_BET rows do not enter ROI capital or P&L.

## Integrity / limitations
- This is **historical validation, not a promise of future ROI**.
- The archive's historical prices are synthetic/reconstructed from model probabilities. They are useful for comparative research but are not equivalent to timestamped bookmaker closing prices.
- The final chronological 20% is a validation slice, not an untouched external dataset; independent live confirmation remains required.
- Performance is not uniformly superior in every sub-period. The purpose of the gate is to improve selection quality over the evaluated regime, not to guarantee every month/quarter.
- No stake escalation or martingale is used to obtain the lift.
